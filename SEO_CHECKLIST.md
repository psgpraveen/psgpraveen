## ⚠️ Common SEO Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Keyword Stuffing**: Don't overuse keywords
2. **Duplicate Content**: Each page should be unique
3. **Slow Page Speed**: Optimize images and code
4. **Missing Meta Descriptions**: Write compelling descriptions
5. **Broken Links**: Regularly check and fix
6. **Ignoring Mobile**: Mobile-first is essential
7. **No Analytics**: Always track your performance
8. **Neglecting Local SEO**: Important for local businesses
9. **Poor User Experience**: Focus on UX
10. **Not Updating Content**: Keep content fresh
